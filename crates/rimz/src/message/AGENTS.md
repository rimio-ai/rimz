# Message system

Local contract for `crates/rimz/src/message/`: delivery that routes durable text into a running agent's pane. Extends [crates/rimz/AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

Topic detail lives in [messaging.md](../../../../docs/internals/harness/messaging.md), which owns the record, the ordered delivery check, the pipeline, the shared turn reader, and channels; the transcript log and asks are [transcript.md](../../../../docs/internals/harness/transcript.md).

## Invariants

- **Sender notices are once-only.** Agent senders are told once per undelivered ending and once per long-queued state; harness senders never are, so a notice cannot cause a notice.
- **The record is the message.** Every send persists a `MessageRecord` before a byte reaches a pane. A pane write is an attempt against that record, never the message itself.
- **`Sent` precedes the submit.** `write_text` records the batch as `Sent` after the text lands (the whole paste, or every raw-typed segment of a command) and before it presses Enter, so a submitted message always has a durable record and audit event behind it. A record a cancel, clear, or archive finalized during the write stays terminal: `write_text` warns and still presses Enter.
- **One pane, one writer.** Every RimZ pane write (`message`, `answer`, `pane send`) runs inside `mux::PaneWriter`, one per-pane room-state advisory lock held across the entire write batch, from the first byte through Enter, including compact-first commands and paced segments. A fresh message send atomically enqueues and claims its record before writing, with a boundary send checking its card's lane in that same commit and parking `Queued` behind any blocker instead; a compact-first command atomically enqueues and claims its record. Steer waits for an in-flight write and the typing hold; it writes into the live turn without disrupting another writer. Interrupt holds its one writer from the paste through Enter, the interrupt delay, and the interrupt key, so no other write lands between its prompt and its key.
- **One card, one FIFO queue.** Records key on `(kind, agent_id)` with `agent_name` folding a provisional `launch_*` id into the session it registers as. `msg_` id string order is FIFO order; a fresh boundary send assigns its id inside its claim commit, after every record live on the card, and `Resume` control messages occupy a separate lane. One exception: a cache keepalive's forced fire (`MessageRecord.forced_keepalive`) claims ahead of the queued records on its card, which keep their statuses, stored holds and order, while a claimed or `Sent` record ahead of it still holds it, and once claimed or sent it holds the records it passed. A `Sent` prompt other than a soft-compaction wrap-up keeps its FIFO slot until acknowledged or its reconcile deadline passes, including any compaction, ingress, or provider-start deferral. FIFO readers release an overdue hold without changing the record; reconciliation still requeues or times it out.
- **Soft-compaction wrap-ups are steer-only.** The optional `soft_compact` tag is `wrap_up` or `continue` on the record and its event payload, omitted when absent. A wrap-up is never a FIFO head, batch member, blocker, or held slot. Reconciliation preserves a queued wrap-up for the claim TTL and a claimed one until its claim expires, then cancels rather than retrying it. At a sent wrap-up's deadline it silently holds while the receiver owns an open turn; otherwise it settles `Delivered` when `turn_ended_at` is at or after its last write, or `TimedOut`. It never requeues or resends, and operator requeue refuses it. Ordinary prompt acknowledgement still confirms it. A continue uses the ordinary boundary FIFO, but delivery cancels it when its live card no longer carries a soft-compaction mark, before candidacy on the head and after claim on every batch member.
- **Acknowledgements follow submitted text.** A submit that contains an intact headered record confirms it. Stray composer text around RimZ's envelope is direct input and is never a reason to write the pane again; headerless system records confirm only on an exact whole-prompt match, and reported text that contains no record confirms nothing. A late acknowledgement stays valid while the record is `Queued`; only a new claim supersedes it.
- **A queued record follows its card.** A `Queued` record carries no pane: each attempt binds the pane its card holds then (`address::bind_agent`), and `pane_id` names only the pane a claim writes to, kept as history on `Sent` and on an `Errored` record that a failed write ended. Delivery ignores a pane an older record still carries, so a restart that replaces the pane strands no record and blocks none behind it.
- **Commands reach the pane at most once.** An unconfirmed command times out without a resend because duplicate commands such as `/compact` can destroy context.
- **Automatic commands are perishable.** A queued `Command` with `automated: true` expires ten minutes after `enqueued_at`. Read-time expiry belongs to `MessageRecord::is_deliverable`, so every FIFO head and blocker ignores it without a helper or a reader-local guard. `Store::reconcile_stale_messages` alone writes `Expired` and its audit reason. It also expires a queued resume follow-up at or after `register_by` only while its card is absent or every matching row ended no later than `enqueued_at`; a later end instead permits receiver-ended archival. That card-aware rule never enters pure record expiry. A follow-up joining an unexpired registration wait on its card shares that wait's `register_by`, decided in the queue commit. Other prompts and operator commands never expire.
- **Unconfirmed writes can be late, not lost.** Outside the steer-only wrap-up rule, the composer queues a write during compaction; reconciliation holds the `Sent` record while the bracket is open, and the last armed deadline survives its close. Wake-in-flight readers use the same open bracket to hold a `Sent` harness wake regardless of its deadline or the 90 s display/delivery window. Reconciliation also holds prompts and commands while an unapplied hook frame of the same provider kind is timestamped at or after the last write and before last write + body window, regardless of any deferral. Prompts to a starting receiver get the bounded resume hold below. These holds move no counters and emit no audit event. An ingress hold records the hook-drain lag in `last_error`; CLI message sweep and gc start or nudge the drainer on that hold, warning on wake failure.
- **Two counters, two caps.** `attempts` guards pre-send claim failures (`MAX_DELIVERY_ATTEMPTS`); for prompts, `unconfirmed_sends` guards writes a lifecycle hook never confirmed (`DEFAULT_MAX_DELIVERY_ATTEMPTS`, overridable per run through the `RIMZ_MESSAGE_MAX_DELIVERY_ATTEMPTS` environment variable). Keep them separate.
- **Delivery reads store state, except the typing hold.** Gates evaluate the rollup, the message queue, and provider-park evidence from capacity caches, park records, and resume outcomes; a subagent digest additionally reads the run rows linked to it, and a run-linked record reads its named run. The typing hold alone reads the room's runtime typing record, the pane's tty access time on Linux, and mux client state through `MuxBackend::client_view`, before waking or locking the pane and again after pacing under the pane write lock. Captured composer contents never decide a delivery.
- **Joined digests are checked before and after claim.** Both durable run scans suppress delivery when a digest has linked rows and all are joined, even if producer cleanup already removed the live message. Digests batch like any prompt, since the post-claim check runs on every claimed member; an unanswered guard never sends and releases the whole claimed set. A join after the final check can still race with external pane I/O; [messaging.md](../../../../docs/internals/harness/messaging.md#the-subagent-digest-join-guard) owns the guard and recovery mechanics.
- **A claimed batch never fails as a whole on its head's account.** A stop decided from the head alone settles terminally only the records that decision is about, and returns every other claimed member to `Queued` with no attempt counted: the cold-cache stop errors the keepalive records and releases the rest. A change to what may share a batch reads every terminal `record_message_delivery_failures` call in `execute_attempt` against the new members.
- **Run-linked notices retire at delivery.** Before candidacy on the head and after claim on every batch member, a terminal or missing `about_run` cancels the record and skips its pane write; a `HarnessNotice::Deadline` stop is also canceled when its open run no longer holds a claimed stop rung (`harness::deadline::stop_claimed`). Run-read errors other than not-found warn and deliver without releasing the claim, so advisory notices cannot hold the FIFO lane on unreadable run state. Batch survivors alone determine their `batch_id`; [messaging.md](../../../../docs/internals/harness/messaging.md#the-run-linked-notice-guard) owns the gaps and error policies.
- **Idle compactions retire after a new turn.** Only the idle helper sets the idle flag; `send_compact` stamps `idle_since` from its pre-wake decision row's turn edge, the later of `turn_started_at` and `turn_ended_at`. Before candidacy on the head and after claim on every member, delivery cancels a queued or claimed tagged record when its live card's edge is strictly later. A reset before the decision is included in the stamp; a resumed parked turn counts at Stop. Ties and keepalive pings keep it. Flip, compact-first and manual commands remain untagged; [messaging.md](../../../../docs/internals/harness/messaging.md#the-idle-compaction-turn-guard) owns the guard and its gaps.
- **`retry_after` is a wake hint.** It schedules the host's next look and never affects `is_ready` or claim leases. One exception: a compaction, ingress, or provider-start deferral on a `Sent` record sets the deadline for reconciliation and readers alike, including FIFO, acknowledgement selection, and hook-driven boundary delivery. Clearing the hold's condition does not shorten that deadline.
- **Resumed providers start before boundary delivery.** A non-lazy provider's resumed row parks boundary deliveries until its next lifecycle observation (normally provider registration) or one delivery window elapses; steer and interrupt bypass this wait. For every kind, reconciliation holds an unconfirmed prompt written at or after the receiver's `resumed_at` while that stamp remains, bounded by ten delivery windows from the resume (five minutes by default). It silently pushes `retry_after` one window ahead, before checking the resend cap, without changing counters or `last_error`. This resend hold includes steer and interrupt, but never commands or writes from before the resume. Window zero holds nothing; release still honours the last armed deadline.

## Boundaries

- Layering runs one way: `dispatch` calls `deliver` and `send`; `deliver` calls `send`; `send` calls the store and the mux. Nothing calls back up.
- `message.rs` owns delivery assembly and parsing. The record schema and FIFO/claim/batch selection live in `store::message`, while I/O delivery belongs in the submodules.
- `store::message::classify_submitted_prompt` owns submitted-prompt origin for transcript and spend consumers. Classification is read-only over confirmed records and the card's in-flight queue view, read before acknowledgement; it never finalizes a record. The ack contract is unchanged: `TurnStarted` confirms only prompts, `Compaction` only commands, so a system command can be classified as harness while still `Sent`.
- Status transitions stay in `store/writer/queue.rs`, under the workspace lock, each with its audit event. The status enum carries vocabulary, not rules.
- Message content never enters the event log. Terminal text lives in `messages/history.jsonl`, which is audit: the live queue commits ahead of it, and a failed history append or retention pass warns without undoing the transition.
- `fire.rs` runs on the renderer's cache-refresh tick, so keep it as light as that path demands. It reads the wake stamp and spawns `message sweep`, naming the room by workspace id and mux in argv; store reads and writes stay in that helper. The refresh loop remembers the last spawned stamp and that pass's time: an unchanged due stamp waits one prompt delivery window, while a changed due stamp wakes immediately. Deliverable ordinary queued followers arm expiry and future registration deadlines, not their ordinary retry or ready backstop. A future typing hold also arms its deadline, even on followers or Resume records; after expiry, only a replay or Resume hold adds its moved `retry_after`, or its past deadline once an edit or released claim has cleared that hint. The store re-arms the next head in the queue commit that moves its predecessor.
- The agent-address grammar, its renderer, pane binding, and the launch-instance grouping with its lineage projections live in the top-level `address` module at the message layer; `message`, `harness`, the CLI, and one verdicted `agents` renderer site reach it, and `address` reaches only `agents`, `store`, `ids`, and `utils` (the list selector's name suggestion and duration parsing). This module resolves targets through it and never parses addresses itself.
- CLI handlers own flag parsing, rendering, and exit codes. Dispatch conditions, delivery causality, and turn-completion reading live here.
- `compact.rs` owns standalone native compaction for operator, idle-compaction and flip callers, including repeat refusal and boundary delivery and wake arming through `synthetic`. Repeat refusal ignores a queued idle compaction whose decision lapsed after a new turn, leaving cancellation to delivery; a claimed one still refuses.
- `soft_compact.rs` owns the explicit wrap-up steer and the continue's queue-tail boundary attempt. A missed wrap-up is canceled with its reason only while still queued; another sender's claim is left alone. Continue carries the seat's smart line into the existing compact-first path, so it needs no separately queued compact command. Compact-first skips its command, and writes the prompt uncompacted, while the receiver's login reads a spent model sub-cap, or a spent 5-hour or weekly window that no usable paid extra usage carries (`agents::ProviderCapacity::login_compaction_blocked`).
- `synthetic.rs` owns RimZ-authored text for a card: the record's channel and sender, the boundary attempt on the multiplexer of the pane its caller read, and the recorded miss. A pre-claim refusal records the verdict's reason; a post-claim stop preserves the attempt's reason under `Retry`, while `Terminal` uses the caller's fallback to error the record. A typing hold preserves its queued record under `Retry`; terminal cache pings and steer-only wrap-ups keep their miss contracts, and a cache ping's forced fire (`MessageRecord.forced_keepalive`) skips the typing hold and its place in the card's queue, and no other gate. An error records its own sentence. A miss settles only a `Queued` record and leaves another sender's claim alone; each queue write refreshes the host wake inside the store commit. A queued `Resume` record ordinarily arms no wake: the sweep's `queue_head` excludes its control lane, and auto-continue owns its re-drive. A typing-held resume instead arms its future hold deadline, then its moved retry deadline, and the sweep retries it independently with its resume gate intact. Claimed and sent resume records still arm recovery. The `cli` helpers that spawn for a keepalive, an auto-continue, a verify re-prompt or a budget continuation resolve their target, call it, and keep only the assist record and the exit code.
