List what you started that is alive, or done with its report still owed to you: your subagents, the agents and teams you launched, workflow runs, schedules you booked, and waits you armed, with what the agents you started started in turn. Each row has `id` (`kind:name`, which `join` and `stop` take), `kind`, `status` (`running`; `done`, ended with its report owed; `disabled`, a disabled schedule), `on_your_stop` (`holds` keeps a stop of you waiting, `closes` ends with you, `outlives` runs on after you), and when known `age`, `about`, and `detail`. `kind` keeps rows of one kind. A row is a thing you started, not a loop task: a loop task you booked is a row of kind `schedule`. Your provider's own background tasks and native subagents are not listed.
