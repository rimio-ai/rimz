Launch a team: several agents on one pipeline in their own worktree, which plan, build, and review a change and report back as one when the board reaches Done. Use it for a change a seat that did not write it should review before it lands, a behavior-preserving cleanup pass, or a test-suite cut.

`team` is the one the user named. Otherwise `rimz teams list` lists the configured teams: `forge` is the usual default, `spot` a minor fix, `recon` a change that still needs design. `prompt` is the brief and goes to the leader: the goal, the files, the constraints, and how done is judged. Give exactly one of `worktree`, a kebab-case name of at most two words, and `from_pr`, a number or URL. A new worktree branches from `agents.worktree.base` when set, else from origin's default branch, fetched at launch, so what the team must see has to be there; a repository with no origin branches from `HEAD`.

The answer is the id `team:<team>#<worktree>`, and `report` says how the result comes: a `TEAM_REPORT` message each time the board reaches Done, or that none comes and `join` is the way to the answer. A run usually takes hours: end your turn. `tasks` lists it, `join` blocks on it, and `stop` ends every seat and leaves the checkout held; `rimz teams show <team>#<worktree>` shows its stage and `rimz teams resume <team>#<worktree>` brings it back. A follow-up is a message to `@<role>#<worktree>`, to the leader unless you mean one seat. Before a first launch, call `guide` with `rimz-teams/launching`.
