Your Agent tool: launch a subagent, a child agent in a pane and context of its own, on one bounded task, and go on at once. Use it for work whose churn you do not want in your window: a task a profile's description matches, independent pieces that run in parallel, or an answer that takes reading across many files when you need only the conclusion. Work you can settle yourself stays with you.

`profile` is one of the profiles your Subagents reminder lists; pick the one whose description matches the task. `prompt` is the brief, the only context the child has: the goal, the files worth reading by path, the scope (read-only, or which files it may edit, since children share your checkout), and exactly what to return. `description` labels the child for the user. Leave `model`, `effort`, and `agent` off unless the user asks for one, and `cwd` off unless the child must work in another checkout. A child is cut off at `timeout`, else at its profile's timeout, 30 minutes by default. Before you write a brief, call `guide` with `rimz-subagents/delegating`.

The answer is the child's id, `subagent:<petname>`, with `warnings` when the launch changed or skipped something you asked for. Its result comes to you as a `SUBAGENT_REPORT` message once every child you have running has settled, naming the file that holds each final message; keep working or end your turn meanwhile. `join` blocks on the one result your next step cannot start without, `tasks` lists your children, and `stop` ends one. A follow-up question goes to the same child as a message to `@<petname>`, which keeps its reading; a different task is a fresh child.
