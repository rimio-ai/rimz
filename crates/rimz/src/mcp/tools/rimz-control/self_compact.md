When the user asks, compact your own context with the configured brief, or with the `instruction` you give in its place, which adapters whose command takes no instruction (such as Codex and Antigravity) refuse. Called in this turn, the compaction runs first when the turn ends, ahead of any prompts waiting in your queue, and you are then prompted to go on with your work if nothing else is waiting; `no_continue` leaves you at rest instead. A compaction already queued for you is joined, not repeated.
