When the user wants your pane closed, book your own stop: the pane closes once you have rested `when_idle` (3m by default, longer when the user needs time to read your final reply), counted from the end of your last turn, with nothing owed to you; `off` withdraws the booking. Book it in the turn that finishes the work, even when the user says now, and say in that reply that the pane closes after the rest. Withdraw it with `off` when more work turns up. A prompt from the user withdraws the booking, so book again in your answer when the user still wants the pane closed. A delivery from rimz (a report, a wait, a stage flip) restarts the rest, and the stop waits while something is owed to you: a subagent or agent you launched that is mid-turn, an armed wait, a background shell, an open question to the user. Cancel the waits (`wait_cancel`) and stop the background shells the finished work no longer needs.
