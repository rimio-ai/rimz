When the user wants your pane closed once your work is over, book your own stop: the pane closes after `when_idle` of rest (`3m` when absent; a longer rest, in `s`, `m`, `h`, or `d`, when the user needs longer to read your final reply), counted from the end of your last turn; `off` withdraws the booking. Book it in the turn that finishes the work, even when the user says now, and say in that reply that the pane closes after the rest. Withdraw it with `off` when more work turns up before the user writes.

A prompt from the user withdraws the booking, so book again in your answer when the user still wants the pane closed. A delivery from rimz (a report, a wait, a stage flip) restarts the rest. The stop also waits while something is owed to you: a subagent or agent you launched that is mid-turn, an armed wait, a background shell, an open question to the user. Cancel the waits (`wait_cancel`) and stop the background shells the finished work no longer needs.
