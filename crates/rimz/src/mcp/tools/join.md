Block on one thing you started until it finishes or `timeout` passes, returning its outcome as a value, never an error: `status: "done"` with `result` (a subagent's result, an agent's final message, a team's board `Result` section, or a workflow run's notes path when it has one) and `detail` (how it ended; for a workflow run, its end state and checkout), or `status: "running"` at timeout, which leaves it running so you can join again or `stop` it. A thing already done returns `done` with its result. A `shell` blocks until its command exits and answers its outcome, which then comes as no message. A schedule or a watch returns at once with its `status` and `report` rather than blocking (`disabled` for a switched-off schedule; `ended` when a schedule's record or a team's seats are gone). A prompt queued for you ends the join early as `running` with a `hint`: read it, then join again. A subagent you message after it finished answers as a new message to you; join returns its previous result until the resumed turn settles.
