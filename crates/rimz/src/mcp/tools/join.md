Block on one thing you started, by its `id` as `tasks` or a start answered it, until it is done or `timeout` passes (`4m` when absent, at most `9m`), and answer its result as a value, never as an error: `{id, status: "done", result}`, or `{id, status: "running"}` at the timeout, which leaves it running, so join again or `stop` it. `result` is a subagent's or agent's final message, a team's board `Result` section, or a workflow run's notes path (absent when it has none), and `detail` says how it ended (for a workflow run, its end state and checkout). A thing that ended before the call answers `done` with its result. A schedule or a wait answers at once with its `status` and a `report` saying its outcome comes to you as a message, except a switched-off schedule, which answers `disabled`, since nothing comes while it stays off; one whose record is gone answers `ended`, as a team whose seats are gone does. A prompt queued for you ends the join early as `running` with a `hint`: read it, then join again.
