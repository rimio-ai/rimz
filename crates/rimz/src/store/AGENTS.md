# Store

Local contract for `crates/rimz/src/store/` — durable workspace state. Extends [crates/rimz/AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md). Subsystem behaviour — on-disk shape, event log, write and read paths, write classes, maintenance — lives in [docs/internals/store.md](../../../../docs/internals/store.md).

Store folds the agent model, so `store` imports `agents` and nothing under `agents` imports `store`; the durable message record, its codec, and its FIFO/claim selection live in `store::message`, and `message/` is delivery that imports them.

## Writes and durability

- Every store mutator and its public intent/outcome vocabulary lands under [`writer.rs`](./writer.rs): the façade owns the ordinary commit and log-boundary primitives, and `writer/` owns the debounce, lifecycle, publish, reset, queue, and reap branches. **`store` owns every record it persists, reaches nothing in `harness`, and reaches `diag` downward for the bind diagnostics it constructs; disk mechanics, usage measurement included, sit below it in `disk`; `harness` is policy over store records and reaches down.** The supervised-run record lives in [`run.rs`](./run.rs): schema, durable codec, `RunRecord::mark_terminal`, and the terminal wake sender; `harness::run` owns the transitions and their workspace lock, and `harness::run_wake` binds the waiter that sender reaches. The signal vocabulary the event log persists lives in [`event.rs`](./event.rs) beside its payload; `harness::schedule` owns selectors, watch outcomes and firing. Reads on the `Store` handle stay lock-free, with one exception: `list_messages_and_history` reads the live queue and the message history under the workspace lock, because a terminal transition writes the two files as one step under that lock and the stage re-wake's de-duplication needs a view in which a record is in exactly one. Snapshot schema is consumed through its owning module.
- `workspace.lock` serializes store mutations by short-lived CLI writers and the elected, idle-exiting hook drainer. [`ingress.rs`](./ingress.rs) owns raw hook frames and the drain cursor: appends hold the caller-owned `locks/hook-ingress.lock` shared and blocking through the distinct `SharedAppendLock` guard; cursor writes hold the caller-owned workspace lock; repair, truncate-and-reset and room reset hold both, workspace then exclusive ingress, never the reverse. The queue reconciler reads pending ingress under the workspace lock, taking ingress only for a length snapshot. The drainer's repair read excludes appenders while decoding and cutting a torn tail, then releases ingress before application; corrupt terminated rows are skipped with durable diagnostic evidence. GC and event-log rotation leave the log and cursor alone, and the lock sweep keeps a held shared ingress lock.
- Every durable write routes through [`disk/atomic.rs`](../disk/atomic.rs), and every fsync syscall lives inside it; `cargo xtask invariants` rejects a `sync_all`/`sync_data` call anywhere else. No module hand-rolls its own atomic dance, and the event-log frame encoding stays beside its decoder in [`event_log/frame.rs`](./event_log/frame.rs). Rotation, archive retention, repair, batch append and log replace are this module's own write path and stay inside it; what the rest of the crate spells is the framed read, the single-record append, the extent stamp, the error and the byte counters.
- [`message.rs`](./message.rs) owns the durable record vocabulary, submitted-prompt header grammar (`HeaderKind`, `parse_message_header`, `split_batched_prompt`), the submitted-prompt classifier (`classify_submitted_prompt`, returning sections typed by `transcript`'s `SectionOrigin`), the conversation/system split (`MessageSender::is_conversation`), FIFO/claim/batch selection, and `align_submitted_prompt`, which maps one submitted pane prompt back onto the records written as its batch; it strips the headers `address` composes, so both spell a notice's type through `HarnessNotice::header_type`. The header reader derives named notices from the same `HarnessNotice` vocabulary and accepts unknown uppercase notice types only from exactly `From: @rimz`. Classification is read-only over confirmed records and the card's in-flight queue view, read through `list_messages` before acknowledgement; it never changes status. The acknowledgement contract stays body-specific: `TurnStarted` confirms prompts, `Compaction` confirms commands. [`message/codec.rs`](./message/codec.rs) owns the live queue and history codec. [`writer/queue.rs`](./writer/queue.rs) owns all three message surfaces together: the live `messages.jsonl`, terminal text in time buckets under `audit/messages/`, and terminal outcomes in the event log, with status transitions under the workspace lock and their audit events. Archive retention stays with event-log rotation, global orphan-temp collection with GC, and cache-local temp-sibling cleanup with [`disk/atomic.rs`](../disk/atomic.rs). [`message/sender_notice.rs`](./message/sender_notice.rs) owns sender resolution and sender-notice prose; `QueueTxn::close` observes qualifying undelivered endings for `Store::commit_queue` to queue in the same commit, while long-queued notices stamp `queued_notice_at` and enqueue together under the same lock.
- A type carried in an event's params grows only by optional, defaulted fields. A new enum variant or a required field makes an older binary decode the whole event as `Other` and drop it from every fold, and worktrees of a project share one store, so mixed binaries are the ordinary case ([what the log holds](../../../../docs/internals/store.md#what-is-in-it)).
- Group sync and dead-session reap stamps stay in state; checkpoint publication paces through runtime `snapshots/publish.stamp`. Appends stay O(1) over log history; each staged hook lifecycle batch uses one ordered-batch append under the workspace lock, with group sync off-lock. Wakeups fire before the publish: consumers fold the log tail from their own cursor, so checkpoint cadence is latency tuning and never truth.
- Every live-queue file write refreshes the message wake stamp immediately afterward, under the same workspace lock, from the records just written and in the store's paired runtime. This includes workspace identity rewrites and staged hook transactions. A refresh failure warns and never fails the queue commit; a no-op commit leaves the stamp alone. The delivery sweep alone requests a locked clock-only rearm, because a queued record's deadline changes when its schedule floor passes.
- The wire is the leaf [`wakeup/`](../wakeup/mod.rs) module below `store`; every sender reaches down to it (this module's write tail, `mux`, `remote_control`, the sidebar graph and its renderer, the CLI), and none reaches another module through it. This module reaches it twice: the post-commit `wake_store_delta` send in [`writer/publish.rs`](./writer/publish.rs), and the heartbeat record GC reads while sweeping expired heartbeats in [`gc/collect.rs`](./gc/collect.rs).
- A new lifetime class, or a change to what reset, teardown or GC removes, rereads the class table, the Reset section and "What survives what" in [docs/internals/store.md](../../../../docs/internals/store.md), plus reset step 2 in [docs/reference/cli/maintenance.md](../../../../docs/reference/cli/maintenance.md). Each of them lists the classes a boundary drops.

## Reads

- `store::message::read_status` is the parked resume monitor's fold-free lookup for one message id. It holds the workspace lock across live queue and all retained history, not the newest-500 history view, so a terminal transition cannot appear missing between the two reads.
- `snapshot/` reducers stay pure over the event log plus caller-supplied inputs. The sidebar's produce module ([`src/sidebar/produce/`](../sidebar/produce/mod.rs)) supplies the live pane list; nothing under `snapshot/` calls the mux.
- `Store::snapshot` folds the event log alone: its `focused_pane` and pane enrichment stay empty, since only the sidebar's producer fuses them in. A decision that needs focus reads the published sidebar snapshot or the mux focus anchor, never a fresh store snapshot.
- The fold stays resumable: carryover plus the rollup cache and its extent stamp preserve continuing and ended rows across rotation, so a rebuild never silently drops launch identity or a resumable session. A change to what the rollup cache or carryover stores keeps `snapshot/fold/tests/integrity.rs`'s `fold_seed_delta_equals_fold_empty_all_over_arbitrary_sequences` (a warm fold over the cache equals a cold fold from empty) passing; run it before any other fold test.
- A field added to a struct the persisted snapshot carries bumps `SNAPSHOT_VERSION` ([`snapshot/view.rs`](./snapshot/view.rs)); a field the rollup cache carries bumps `ROLLUP_CACHE_VERSION` ([`snapshot/fold.rs`](./snapshot/fold.rs)). An `AgentState` field bumps both. Re-check the versions against the trunk after every rebase, even a clean one: when the trunk bumped a version to the value the branch chose, git merges the two bumps into one without a conflict, and each regression test's literal pre-bump stamp still reads as older and passes. A trunk that moved a version since the branch's old base means the branch bumps again past it and moves its pre-bump stamps to the trunk's value.
- A field added to `AgentCard` carries what the renderer reads (a bool or a short label), not the record behind it: the sidebar snapshot is persisted as `latest.json`, reparsed after every publish, and projected for every pane. Explicit agent reads use the rollup row; every row keeps normalized prompt labels of at most 160 characters and the full-text wake classification, ended rows drop pane-command text, and revival continues from those labels.
- Agent and subagent enrichment scans go through [`sidecar.rs`](./sidecar.rs), which gates each scan on stat. Its record trait, parse cache and read/write entry points serve store records only and stay inside the store; the file-name digest is the one name it shares upward.
- A card name stays owned by its row, ended or live, until that row leaves the fold ([`snapshot/project/identity.rs`](./snapshot/project/identity.rs)). A same-kind registration under an owned name adopts the owner's row instead of registering beside it when the owner is provisional or has no pane.
- A test that needs distinct same-name cards sets `parent_agent_id` and `launch.launch_depth` on each observation, or adoption folds them into one row.
- A decision about attention (bucket counts, the pane jump, a projection that overrides a row's status) reads `SidebarRow::attention_status`, not `status()`: a current child's `?` lifts its parent only there.
- [`follow.rs`](./follow.rs) is the read-only lifecycle event-log follower. It folds lifecycle records apart from the rollup, so a rule that changes how a record folds is one function both call: `AgentState::snapshot_transition` for state snapshots and `session_death::reaped_end_rests_at_idle` for reaper ends.
- The rollup catch-up, rebuild, carryover staging and lifecycle-follow seeds under [`snapshot/`](./snapshot/mod.rs) are this module's own read choreography and stay inside it; the snapshot's crate-wide surface is the view model the renderer decodes, beside the pane classifiers, the lazy-pairing helpers, and the per-pane local-session demand (`SidebarSnapshot::pane_session_demands`, built from the same pane facts as the pairing ladder) that the sidebar's producer reads.
- [`disk/single_flight.rs`](../disk/single_flight.rs) stays the lock-and-poll election alone and imports no store-writer module. The sidebar depends on it, and the read-only grep matches files under `src/sidebar/` rather than this one, so the boundary holds only where a change here holds it.

Workflow record and journal codecs live in `workflow.rs`; the harness owns every workflow transition under the workspace lock. Workflow homes use the owned lifetime: soft reset retains them, hard reset removes them, and terminal-record GC removes the whole home after the owned grace, and so does a home left without its record past the same grace.

## Tests

Durability behaviour — CAS round trips, torn-record recovery, rotation, the write path — lives in [`tests/integration/store/`](../../tests/integration/store/); pure reducer and helper tests stay in-module. Time is fixed-epoch in fixtures, except lifecycle events a test reads back through `Store::snapshot()`: the snapshot reaps pidless rows idle past three hours, so those stamps stay near now.

A test that forges a replacement's size or mtime to prove an identity check sees the inode change holds each replaced file open: ext4 hands a freed inode to the next file at once, while the dev host's ZFS does not, so the test passes locally and fails in CI.
